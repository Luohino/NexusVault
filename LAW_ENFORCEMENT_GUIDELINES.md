# Law Enforcement Guidelines

NexusVault is committed to user privacy and transparency. These guidelines provide information to law enforcement and government agencies regarding how we handle requests for user data.

## 1. Respect for User Rights
We will only provide non-public user data in response to a valid legal process (e.g., a subpoena, court order, or warrant). We review every request for legal sufficiency and will challenge those that are overbroad or procedurally deficient.

## 2. Requirements for a Valid Request
A valid request must:
- Be in writing and on official agency letterhead.
- Be signed by a responsible official.
- Specifically identify the user account or repository in question.
- Cite the legal authority under which the request is made.

## 3. User Notification Policy
Unless prohibited by law or a valid court order (e.g., a non-disclosure order), we notify users when we receive a request for their data. This allows users the opportunity to challenge the request before their data is disclosed.

## 4. Emergency Requests
In extraordinary circumstances involving a risk of immediate death or serious physical injury, NexusVault may disclose information to law enforcement without a warrant. Such requests must be submitted via email to **lutervyn@gmail.com** with the subject line "EMERGENCY DATA REQUEST."

## 5. Cost Reimbursement
NexusVault reserves the right to seek reimbursement for costs associated with responding to legal requests for information, as permitted by law.
