# Anti-Corruption & Ethics Policy

NexusVault operates on the principles of transparency and technical merit. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for bribery, corruption, and unethical business practices.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to Luohino, all platform maintainers, and any third-party contractors acting on behalf of NexusVault.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct
*   **Bribery:** Offering, promising, giving, or accepting any financial or other advantage in exchange for platform influence (e.g., prioritized issue resolution, unauthorized repository access, or biased moderation).
*   **Conflicts of Interest:** Maintainers must disclose any personal or financial interests that could conflict with their responsibilities to the NexusVault community.
*   **Data Exploitation:** The unauthorized sale or trading of user metadata for personal gain is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Political Neutrality
NexusVault is a neutral technical infrastructure. We do not make political contributions or allow the platform's official branding to be used for partisan political purposes.

## 4. Reporting
Any suspected violations of this policy should be reported immediately to **lutervyn@gmail.com**. All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality.
