# Moderation Guidelines

NexusVault is a self-governed anomaly network. These guidelines outline how Luohino and designated moderators (Sentinels) manage content and community disputes.

## 1. Our Philosophy
We believe in **Maximum Expression, Minimum Harm**. We prefer to allow developers to build freely, only intervening when content violates our [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) or [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## 2. Moderation Actions
Depending on the severity of the violation, we may take the following actions:
*   **Warning:** A formal notification to the user to correct their behavior.
*   **Content Removal:** Hiding or deleting specific comments, issues, or wiki pages.
*   **Repository Lockdown:** Making a repository read-only or private.
*   **Account Suspension:** Temporary or permanent loss of access to the platform.

## 3. Reporting Violations
Users are encouraged to report "System Anomalies" (violations) by:
1.  Using the "Report" button on the UI (where available).
2.  Emailing **lutervyn@gmail.com** with evidence (screenshots, URLs).

## 4. Appeals Process
If you believe your content was moderated in error, you may submit a one-time appeal to Luohino. We will review the decision based on the original context and our community standards.

## 5. Transparency
We aim to provide a reason for every moderation action. However, we reserve the right to act without notice in cases of extreme security risk or illegal activity.
