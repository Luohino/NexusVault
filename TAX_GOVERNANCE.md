# Tax Governance & Strategy

NexusVault is committed to high standards of tax compliance and transparency in all jurisdictions where the platform operates.

## 1. Compliance Principles
*   **Accuracy:** We ensure that all tax filings and payments are accurate and submitted on time.
*   **Integrity:** We do not engage in artificial tax planning or use "tax havens" to avoid our fiscal responsibilities.
*   **Transparency:** We maintain an open and honest relationship with tax authorities.

## 2. Risk Management
Tax risks are monitored by the Founding Architect (Luohino). We seek professional advice for any complex international tax issues resulting from the platform's global nature.

## 3. Transfer Pricing
Any future transactions between NexusVault entities (e.g., localized regional hubs) will be conducted on an "arm's length" basis, in accordance with OECD guidelines.

## 4. Reporting
We maintain comprehensive financial records as documented in our [Records Management Policy](RECORDS_MANAGEMENT.md).
