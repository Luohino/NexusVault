# Accessibility Statement

NexusVault is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

## 1. Our Goal
Our goal is to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. We believe the "Anomaly" should be navigable and usable by every developer, regardless of their hardware or physical requirements.

## 2. Measures to Support Accessibility
- We use semantic HTML to ensure screen readers can navigate the repository structure.
- We maintain a high-contrast Neo-Brutalist design to assist users with low vision.
- We ensure keyboard navigability for the core IDE and repository features.
