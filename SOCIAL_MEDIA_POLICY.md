# Social Media & External Communication Policy

NexusVault maintains a distinct brand identity characterized by Neo-Brutalist design and technical radicalism. This policy governs how the platform and its representatives interact with the public.

## 1. Official Channels
The only official social media channels for NexusVault are managed directly by Luohino.
*   **X (Twitter):** Official announcements and release highlights.
*   **Discord/Telegram:** Community support and anomaly discussions.

## 2. Tone & Voice
*   **Technical Excellence:** We prioritize factual, high-fidelity information over marketing hype.
*   **Neo-Brutalist Clarity:** Our communication is direct, high-contrast, and unsentimental.
*   **The Anomaly Lens:** We refer to the platform using the established [Glossary](GLOSSARY.md) terms (Anomalies, Payloads, Sentinels).

## 3. Maintainer Conduct
When maintainers or contributors represent NexusVault on external platforms:
*   **Disclose Affiliation:** Clearly state your role in the project.
*   **Respect Privacy:** Never share non-public user data or internal system secrets.
*   **Follow the Code of Conduct:** All external interactions must adhere to our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## 4. Media Inquiries
For press inquiries, interviews, or branding collaborations, please contact **lutervyn@gmail.com**.
