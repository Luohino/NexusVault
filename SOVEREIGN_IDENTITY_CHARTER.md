# Sovereign Identity Charter

NexusVault believes that your digital identity is yours to own, not ours to profile. This charter outlines our commitment to user autonomy.

## 1. Minimal Data Collection
We collect only the essential metadata required to maintain your account and verify repository ownership. We do not track your activity across other websites or build a social graph of your behavior.

## 2. Identity Portability
You have the right to export your entire identity—including contribution history and metadata—at any time. We will never "lock" your identity into the NexusVault network.

## 3. Right to be Forgotten (Full Purge)
If you choose to leave the anomaly network, we provide a "One-Click Deletion" feature that permanently erases your personal identity from our production databases and backup rotations.

## 4. No Unauthorized Profiling
We do not use your identity or repository data to build profiles for targeted advertising or third-party behavioral analysis.
