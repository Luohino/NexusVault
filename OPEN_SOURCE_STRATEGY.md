# Open Source Participation Strategy

NexusVault is built on the shoulders of giants. This strategy outlines our commitment to the broader open-source ecosystem.

## 1. Upstream Contributions
When we identify bugs or performance opportunities in our core dependencies (e.g., Drizzle, Clerk-SDK, Vite), our policy is to contribute the fixes "Upstream" rather than maintaining local forks.

## 2. Licensing Integrity
We strictly adhere to the licenses of our [Third-Party Dependencies](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md). We do not use "Copyleft" code in a manner that would force the NexusVault core to change its [License](LICENSE).

## 3. Financial Support
As the platform scales and generates revenue, a percentage of proceeds will be allocated to the open-source projects that power the anomaly network via platforms like Open Collective or GitHub Sponsors.

## 4. Community Mentorship
We utilize our [Good First Issue](LABEL_GUIDELINES.md) program to mentor new developers in the art of Neo-Brutalist engineering and professional documentation.
