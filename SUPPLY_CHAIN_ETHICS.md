# Supply Chain Code of Conduct

NexusVault relies on a network of third-party providers to power the anomaly. We choose our technical partners based not only on performance but also on their commitment to ethical standards.

## 1. Labor & Human Rights
We expect our primary infrastructure partners (e.g., Clerk, Supabase, AWS) to comply with all applicable labor laws, including those prohibiting child labor, forced labor, and discrimination in the workplace.

## 2. Data Ethics
Our partners must adhere to rigorous data protection standards (e.g., GDPR, SOC2). We prioritize vendors that provide transparency into their data processing and storage practices.

## 3. Environmental Responsibility
We favor partners that have public, science-based targets for reducing their greenhouse gas emissions and environmental impact.

## 4. Software Integrity
We audit our software supply chain (NPM dependencies) for security vulnerabilities and "Protestware." We prioritize well-maintained, open-source libraries with clear licensing.

## 5. Violation
If a critical partner is found to be in systemic violation of these ethical standards, NexusVault will initiate a migration plan to an alternative "Clean" provider.
