# Privacy by Design (PbD) Framework

NexusVault adheres to the Seven Foundational Principles of Privacy by Design. We believe that privacy is not a feature; it is the default state of the anomaly network.

## 1. Proactive not Reactive
We anticipate and prevent privacy invasive events before they happen. Privacy risks are identified during the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) phase.

## 2. Privacy as the Default Setting
All user accounts are created with maximum privacy settings enabled by default. Users must intentionally opt-in to share non-essential metadata.

## 3. Privacy Embedded into Design
Privacy is an integral part of our [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md). Our database schema utilizes RLS (Row Level Security) at the core level to ensure data isolation.

## 4. Full Functionality (Positive-Sum)
We reject the false dichotomy between privacy and performance. NexusVault provides a high-performance experience *without* compromising user anonymity.

## 5. End-to-End Security
Data is protected throughout its entire lifecycle, from the user's local "Transmission" to our [Encrypted Backups](DRP.md).

## 6. Visibility and Transparency
We keep it open. All data practices are documented in our 70+ file governance suite.

## 7. Respect for User Privacy
The user is the sovereign of their data. We provide tools for [Data Portability](DATA_PORTABILITY_GUIDE.md) and the [Right to be Forgotten](SOVEREIGN_IDENTITY_CHARTER.md).
