# Whistleblower Policy

NexusVault is built on the integrity of the anomaly. We encourage all users and contributors to speak up if they observe illegal activity, security negligence, or ethical misconduct within the platform's leadership or community.

## 1. Scope of Reporting
You should report concerns regarding:
*   Intentional security backdoors or data leaks.
*   Theft or misappropriation of platform assets.
*   Violations of the [Anti-Corruption Policy](ANTI_CORRUPTION_POLICY.md).
*   Systemic harassment or discrimination.

## 2. Reporting Channel
Reports should be sent to the dedicated integrity address:
**Email:** **lutervyn@gmail.com** (Subject: INTEGRITY REPORT)

## 3. Confidentiality & Non-Retaliation
*   **Anonymity:** We respect your request for anonymity to the fullest extent permitted by law.
*   **Protection:** NexusVault maintains a strict **No-Retaliation Policy**. No user or contributor will be penalized for making a report in good faith.

## 4. Investigation Process
Every report is reviewed personally by Luohino. If the report involves the lead developer, we will engage an independent community moderator to review the evidence.

## 5. False Reports
Making an intentionally false report is a violation of our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and may lead to account suspension.

**Integrity First.**
