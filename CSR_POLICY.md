# Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Policy

**Last Updated:** April 25, 2026

NexusVault is more than a technical infrastructure; it is a social entity with a responsibility to the global community. This policy outlines our commitment to ethical, social, and environmental excellence.

## 1. Ethical Governance
We operate with radical transparency. Every major decision regarding platform direction, data usage, and community management is documented in our [Transparency Report](TRANSPARENCY_REPORT.md).

## 2. Community Investment
NexusVault commits to providing free high-performance hosting for open-source "Anomalies" that address global challenges, including climate change, public health, and digital education.

## 3. Environmental Stewardship
We prioritize "Green Coding" (as documented in our [Environmental Impact Policy](ENVIRONMENTAL_IMPACT.md)) to minimize the carbon footprint of our software. We favor technical partners who have committed to net-zero operations.

## 4. Human Rights & Labor
We strictly adhere to the [UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights](HUMAN_RIGHTS_POLICY.md) and ensure that our technical supply chain is free from forced labor ([Modern Slavery Statement](MODERN_SLAVERY_STATEMENT.md)).

## 5. Digital Equity
We believe that advanced developer tools should be accessible to everyone. We maintain strict [Accessibility Standards](ACCESSIBILITY_AUDIT.md) to ensure the platform is usable by developers in all regions and with all abilities.
