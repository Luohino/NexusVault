# Stakeholder Engagement Policy

NexusVault recognizes that its success depends on the trust and collaboration of its diverse stakeholders. This policy outlines how we engage with each group.

## 1. Stakeholder Groups
We have identified four primary stakeholder groups:
*   **Users:** Developers who host their anomalies on our platform.
*   **Cultivators:** Contributors who provide code, documentation, or feedback.
*   **Partners:** Technical service providers (Clerk, Supabase, etc.).
*   **The Public:** The broader technology community and regulatory bodies.

## 2. Engagement Principles
*   **Transparency:** We provide public [Transparency Reports](TRANSPARENCY_REPORT.md).
*   **Inclusivity:** We follow our [Diversity & Inclusion Charter](DIVERSITY_INCLUSION_CHARTER.md).
*   **Responsiveness:** We aim to address community issues and PRs in a timely manner.

## 3. Communication Channels
*   **Technical:** GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, and [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) discussions.
*   **Strategic:** Official [Governance Charter](GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md) and [Manifesto](THE_ANOMALY_MANIFESTO.md).
*   **Urgent:** [Incident Response](INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PLAN.md) notifications and [Support](SUPPORT.md).

## 4. Feedback Integration
Community feedback is formally triaged according to our [Issue Triage Policy](ISSUE_TRIAGE_POLICY.md). Major feature requests that align with the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) are prioritized for development.
