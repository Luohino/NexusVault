# EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) Addendum

**Jurisdiction:** European Union / European Economic Area (EEA)

This Addendum incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for the transfer of personal data from the EEA to third countries.

## 1. Applicability
This Addendum applies to any user residing in the EU/EEA whose data is processed by NexusVault or its subprocessors (e.g., U.S.-based infrastructure providers).

## 2. Incorporation of SCCs
NexusVault hereby incorporates the Module Two (Controller-to-Processor) and Module Three (Processor-to-Processor) clauses of the Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 into our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## 3. Supplementary Measures
We utilize additional technical measures to protect EU data, including:
*   AES-256 encryption at rest.
*   TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit.
*   Rigorous subprocessor auditing as defined in [SUBPROCESSORS.md](SUBPROCESSORS.md).
