# Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) Policy

NexusVault performs a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for any new technical "Anomaly" or feature that is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of our users.

## 1. Trigger Conditions
A DPIA is mandatory if a feature involves:
*   Large-scale processing of sensitive user metadata.
*   Automated decision-making or profiling (per our [Ethical AI Policy](ETHICAL_AI_POLICY.md)).
*   New technologies that change the way user payloads are encrypted or stored.

## 2. Assessment Process
The DPIA must include:
1.  **Description:** A detailed flow of the data processing activity.
2.  **Necessity:** An assessment of why the data processing is required for the feature.
3.  **Risk Assessment:** Identification of potential risks to user privacy.
4.  **Mitigation:** The specific technical measures (e.g., pseudonymization, encryption) used to reduce those risks.

## 3. Approval
All DPIAs must be reviewed and approved by the Founding Architect (Luohino) before the feature can be merged into the production branch.

## 4. Documentation
Approved assessments are archived in our internal [Governance Registry](GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md).
