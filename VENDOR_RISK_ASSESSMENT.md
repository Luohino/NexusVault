# Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA) Policy

This policy outlines how NexusVault evaluates and manages risks associated with third-party technical partners and subprocessors.

## 1. Assessment Criteria
Before integrating a new technical partner (e.g., a new AI provider or data engine), we evaluate:
*   **Security:** Does the partner provide SOC2, ISO 27001, or equivalent certifications?
*   **Privacy:** Does the partner's privacy policy align with our [PbD Framework](PRIVACY_BY_DESIGN.md)?
*   **Ethics:** Does the partner have a public commitment to [Human Rights](HUMAN_RIGHTS_POLICY.md)?
*   **Stability:** What is the partner's historical uptime and financial stability?

## 2. Risk Rating
Partners are rated as **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** risk based on the sensitivity of the data they process.

## 3. Continuous Monitoring
We perform an annual review of our primary [Subprocessors](SUBPROCESSORS.md) to ensure they continue to meet our standards.

## 4. Termination & Exit
We maintain an exit strategy for all "High Risk" partners to ensure we can migrate user data and maintain service continuity.
