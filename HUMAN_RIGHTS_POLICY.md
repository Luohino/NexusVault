# Human Rights Policy

**Alignment:** UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights

NexusVault is committed to respecting and promoting human rights across the Anomaly Network. We recognize that digital platforms play a critical role in the protection of fundamental freedoms.

## 1. Respect for Freedom of Expression
We believe that every developer has the right to express their technical vision. We only restrict content that violates our [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) (e.g., malware, harassment, or illegal activity).

## 2. Right to Privacy
Privacy is a fundamental human right. Our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) and [Sovereign Identity Charter](SOVEREIGN_IDENTITY_CHARTER.md) are designed to ensure that users retain control over their digital existence.

## 3. Freedom from Discrimination
We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for discrimination and harassment, as outlined in our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Diversity & Inclusion Charter](DIVERSITY_INCLUSION_CHARTER.md).

## 4. Ethical Supply Chain
We strive to ensure that our technical partners and subprocessors do not engage in human rights abuses. This is codified in our [Supply Chain Ethics](SUPPLY_CHAIN_ETHICS.md) and [Modern Slavery Statement](MODERN_SLAVERY_STATEMENT.md).

## 5. Remedies
Users who believe their human rights have been impacted by the NexusVault platform are encouraged to use the [Whistleblower Policy](WHISTLEBLOWER_POLICY.md) or contact **lutervyn@gmail.com**.
