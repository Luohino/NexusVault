# Diversity & Inclusion Charter

The NexusVault anomaly is powered by the collective intelligence of a global developer community. We believe that progress is only possible when every voice is heard, respected, and empowered.

## 1. Our Principles
*   **Equity over Equality:** We recognize that different contributors face different barriers. We strive to provide the tools and support necessary for everyone to succeed.
*   **Radical Inclusion:** We welcome developers regardless of their background, ethnicity, gender identity, neurodiversity, or physical ability.
*   **Cultural Intelligence:** We respect the global nature of the "Anomaly Network" and encourage localized documentation and communication.

## 2. Inclusive Product Design
*   **Accessibility:** Adhering to WCAG 2.1 Level AA to ensure the platform is usable by developers with disabilities.
*   **Bias Mitigation:** We actively audit our AI features (per our [Ethical AI Policy](ETHICAL_AI_POLICY.md)) to prevent the introduction of systemic bias.
*   **Language:** We favor inclusive, gender-neutral language in our codebase, comments, and documentation.

## 3. Enforcement
Our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) is the primary tool for protecting these values. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for discrimination, harassment, or exclusionary behavior.
