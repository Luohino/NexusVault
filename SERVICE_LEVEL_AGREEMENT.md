# Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026

## 1. Scope
This Service Level Agreement (SLA) applies to the core repository hosting and code browsing services provided by the NexusVault platform.

## 2. Uptime Commitment
NexusVault strives to maintain a **99.9% Monthly Uptime Percentage**. This means the core platform (Login, Repository View, and File Access) should be available and operational for the majority of the time.

## 3. Scheduled Maintenance
We perform periodic system upgrades to improve the anomaly infrastructure. We aim to provide at least 24 hours' notice for any maintenance expected to cause downtime.

## 4. Service Credits
As NexusVault is currently a developer-led initiative, we do not provide monetary service credits for downtime. However, we are committed to transparent reporting and rapid resolution of system failures.

## 5. Exclusions
This SLA does not apply to:
- Issues caused by factors outside of our reasonable control (e.g., global internet outages).
- Failures of third-party providers (e.g., Clerk, Supabase, Vercel).
- Issues resulting from user hardware or local network failures.
