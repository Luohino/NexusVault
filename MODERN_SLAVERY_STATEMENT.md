# Modern Slavery & Human Trafficking Statement

**Reporting Period:** Fiscal Year 2026

This statement is made pursuant to global transparency requirements (including the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 and similar regulations). It outlines the steps taken by NexusVault to ensure that modern slavery and human trafficking are not taking place in our operations or supply chains.

## 1. Our Structure & Operations
NexusVault is a developer-centric software platform. Our operations are primarily digital and involve the cultivation of the "Anomaly Network."

## 2. Our Supply Chain
Our supply chain consists of professional technical service providers, including:
*   **Infrastructure:** Amazon Web Services (AWS), Vercel.
*   **Identity:** Clerk.
*   **Database:** Supabase.

## 3. Our Policies on Slavery and Human Trafficking
We have implemented the following policies to ensure ethical conduct:
*   **[Supply Chain Ethics](SUPPLY_CHAIN_ETHICS.md):** Defines the standards we expect from our technical partners.
*   **[Whistleblower Policy](WHISTLEBLOWER_POLICY.md):** Provides a confidential channel for reporting misconduct.
*   **[Anti-Corruption Policy](ANTI_CORRUPTION_POLICY.md):** Ensures platform integrity.

## 4. Due Diligence
We prioritize partners that have their own public Modern Slavery statements and robust labor compliance programs.

## 5. Training & Awareness
We ensure that all maintainers (Sentinels) are aware of our ethical standards and the importance of maintaining a "Clean" technical supply chain.
