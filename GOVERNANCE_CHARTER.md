# Governance Charter

**Version:** 1.0 (The Sentinel Protocol)
**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026

This Charter defines the decision-making structure, leadership roles, and community interaction models for the NexusVault Anomaly Network.

## 1. Leadership: The Council of Sentinels
The NexusVault project is governed by the "Council of Sentinels."
*   **The Founding Architect (Luohino):** Holds final authority on the platform's core engine, brand identity, and legal protocols.
*   **The Sentinels:** Designated maintainers who have proven their commitment to the network's integrity. Sentinels have the power to review code, triage anomalies, and moderate the community.

## 2. Decision-Making Process
*   **Technical Decisions:** Major changes to the [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) or [Coding Standards](CODING_STANDARDS.md) require a formal RFC (Request for Comments) period of at least 7 days.
*   **Governance Decisions:** Changes to the [Terms](TERMS.md), [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md), or this Charter are made by the Founding Architect with a mandatory 30-day notice period to the community.

## 3. Community Membership: The Cultivators
Any user who contributes code, documentation, or feedback is recognized as a "Cultivator." Cultivators are encouraged to participate in RFCs and propose new "Anomalies" (features).

## 4. Conflict Resolution
Disputes between contributors or Sentinels are resolved by the Founding Architect based on the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and the [Moderation Guidelines](MODERATION_GUIDELINES.md).

## 5. Succession
Leadership succession is managed according to the [Business Continuity Plan](BUSINESS_CONTINUITY_PLAN.md).

**Governance Managed by Luohino.**
