# Export Control & Compliance

## 1. Overview
NexusVault is a platform for software development and distribution. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the platform complies with all applicable export laws and regulations, including those of the United States.

## 2. Prohibited Jurisdictions
You may not use NexusVault if you are located in a country or region that is subject to comprehensive trade sanctions or embargoes (e.g., Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea region of Ukraine).

## 3. Prohibited Parties
You may not use NexusVault if you are listed on any government "denied parties" list, including the U.S. Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list.

## 4. Encryption Export
NexusVault incorporates cryptographic software for data security. Users who upload their own cryptographic software are responsible for complying with the relevant export regulations (e.g., the U.S. Export Administration Regulations).

## 5. Violation
NexusVault reserves the right to terminate accounts found to be in violation of these trade compliance laws.
