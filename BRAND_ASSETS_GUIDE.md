# Brand Assets & Identity Guide

NexusVault is defined by its radical Neo-Brutalist identity. This guide provides the technical specifications for our visual brand assets.

## 1. The Palette (Hex Codes)
*   **Void (Black):** `#000000`
*   **Paper (White):** `#ffffff`
*   **Anomaly (Red):** `#dc2626`
*   **Sentinel (Zinc):** `#71717a`

## 2. The Logo
*   **Symbol:** A stylized "N-V" geometric anomaly.
*   **Clearance:** Maintain a minimum clear space equal to 25% of the logo's width around all sides.
*   **Scaling:** The logo must not be scaled below 32px in width for digital screens.

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary:** Outfit (Google Fonts). Used for headers and branding.
*   **Secondary:** Inter (Google Fonts). Used for body text and technical data.
*   **Monospace:** JetBrains Mono. Used for code payloads and system logs.

## 4. Visual Elements
*   **Hard Shadows:** Always 100% opacity, non-blurred. Standard offset is `8px 8px`.
*   **Borders:** Solid black, `3px` or `4px` width.
*   **Gradients:** Prohibited. NexusVault only utilizes solid color fills.

## 5. Usage Restrictions
*   Do not recolor the "Anomaly Red" to any other shade.
*   Do not add rounded corners to brand-associated containers.
*   Do not blur any element of the brand identity.

**Brand Guardian:** Luohino
