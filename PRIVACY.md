# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026
**Contact:** lutervyn@gmail.com
**Developer:** Luohino

NexusVault is designed with a "Local-First" philosophy, but certain data is synchronized to provide a seamless developer experience across the platform.

## 1. Data Collection
- **Authentication:** Managed by Clerk. We store your email, unique user ID, and profile metadata to verify repository ownership and session security.
- **Repository Metadata:** We store repository names, descriptions, and visibility settings (Public/Private) in our core database.
- **Source Code & Assets:** Code pushed to NexusVault is stored on our secure infrastructure. Private repositories are strictly restricted to the owner's authenticated session.
- **Collaboration Data:** We log commits, issue reports, comments, and pull requests to facilitate the platform's social and technical features.

## 2. Cookies and Tracking
NexusVault uses essential cookies to maintain your authentication session via **Clerk**. These cookies are strictly necessary for the platform to function and cannot be opted out of. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or third-party behavioral analytics.

## 3. Third-Party Subprocessors
To provide our services, we share specific data with the following infrastructure providers:
- **Clerk Inc.**: Handles all identity management, authentication, and session security.
- **Supabase / PostgreSQL**: Stores all repository metadata, user associations, and platform activity.
- **Vercel / AWS**: Provides the core hosting and compute environment for the application.

## 4. Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is only shared when:
- It is required by law or to respond to valid legal processes.
- It is necessary to protect the safety of our users or the integrity of the platform.

## 5. Security Protocols
We implement administrative and technical controls to safeguard your data, including:
- **Encryption at Rest**: Databases and file storage are encrypted.
- **Encryption in Transit**: All traffic is served over HTTPS.
- **Strict Access Control**: RLS (Row Level Security) ensures only you can access your private payloads.

## 6. Your Rights
In accordance with global privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:
- **Access**: View the data we hold about you.
- **Portability**: Request a copy of your data.
- **Deletion**: Request that all your data be permanently purged from our system.

For all inquiries, contact Luohino at **lutervyn@gmail.com**.
