# Gifts, Hospitality & Entertainment Policy

To ensure the integrity of the NexusVault anomaly network, we maintain strict standards regarding the acceptance of gifts and hospitality.

## 1. Core Principle
Decisions made by "Sentinels" and maintainers must be based solely on technical merit and the best interests of the community, never on personal gain.

## 2. Prohibited Gifts
Maintainers may not accept gifts, hospitality, or entertainment that:
*   Could be perceived as a bribe to influence platform influence or prioritize a specific anomaly.
*   Is excessive in value (generally > $100 USD).
*   Consists of cash or cash equivalents.

## 3. Disclosure
Any gift or hospitality received by a maintainer that exceeds $50 USD must be disclosed to the Founding Architect (Luohino) and recorded in our internal [Ethics Registry](ANTI_CORRUPTION_POLICY.md).

## 4. Sponsored Travel
Travel or hospitality sponsored by technical partners (e.g., attending a conference) must be approved in advance and disclosed to the community if it relates to a major platform decision.

**Integrity Policy:** Luohino
