# Community Guidelines

NexusVault is a platform for creators, explorers, and anomaly-hunters. To maintain a productive and respectful environment, we expect all users to follow these guidelines.

## 1. Professionalism & Respect
- **Be Inclusive:** We welcome developers of all skill levels and backgrounds.
- **Constructive Feedback:** Provide helpful reviews and comments. Avoid harassment, hate speech, or derogatory language.
- **Conflict Resolution:** If a dispute arises, remain professional. Contact Luohino if moderation is required.

## 2. Content Quality
- **No Spam:** Do not use issues or comments for advertisement or irrelevant content.
- **Metadata Accuracy:** Provide clear repository names and descriptions to help others navigate the platform.
- **Reporting Anomalies:** Use the issue tracker for its intended purpose: tracking bugs and features.
- **No Malicious Code:** Do not upload or link to code designed to harm, disrupt, or compromise other systems.

## 3. Collaboration Ethics
- **Give Credit:** Respect licenses. If you fork or use code, ensure you follow the original author's terms.
- **Help Others:** NexusVault thrives on shared knowledge. Answering questions and helping with issues is encouraged.

## 4. Enforcement
Luohino reserves the right to remove content or suspend accounts that repeatedly violate these guidelines.

**Stay curious. Build bold.**
