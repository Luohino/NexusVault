# ESG Commitment Report

**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)**

NexusVault is committed to sustainable, ethical, and transparent platform management. This report consolidates our primary ESG strategies.

## 1. Environmental (E)
*   **Green Infrastructure:** 100% reliance on renewable-energy-committed cloud providers.
*   **Efficiency:** Prioritizing "Green Coding" standards to reduce CPU/Energy consumption.
*   *Reference:* [Environmental Impact & Sustainability](ENVIRONMENTAL_IMPACT.md).

## 2. Social (S)
*   **Inclusion:** Maintaining a global, diverse contributor base.
*   **Safety:** Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination.
*   **Privacy:** Protecting user identity and data autonomy.
*   *Reference:* [Diversity & Inclusion Charter](DIVERSITY_INCLUSION_CHARTER.md), [Human Rights Policy](HUMAN_RIGHTS_POLICY.md).

## 3. Governance (G)
*   **Integrity:** Strict anti-corruption and whistleblower protections.
*   **Accountability:** Regular transparency reporting and a formal leadership charter.
*   *Reference:* [Governance Charter](GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md), [Anti-Corruption Policy](ANTI_CORRUPTION_POLICY.md), [Transparency Report](TRANSPARENCY_REPORT.md).

---

NexusVault believes that ethical governance is the foundation of technical innovation.
