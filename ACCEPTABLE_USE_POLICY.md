# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last Updated:** April 25, 2026

NexusVault is a platform for technical excellence and creative collaboration. To ensure a safe environment for all developers, we strictly prohibit the following activities:

## 1. Disruptive Activity
*   **System Abuse:** You may not attempt to bypass rate limits, probe system vulnerabilities, or interfere with the platform's infrastructure.
*   **Automated Scraping:** Unauthorized automated data collection that impacts system performance is prohibited.
*   **Resource Hijacking:** You may not use NexusVault infrastructure for cryptocurrency mining or distributed computing without explicit permission.

## 2. Malicious Content
*   **Malware Distribution:** Hosting or distributing spyware, viruses, ransomware, or any code designed to damage or hijack a third party's system.
*   **Phishing & Fraud:** Using the platform to deceive users into providing personal information or financial data.

## 3. Harassment & Safety
*   **Hate Speech:** Content that promotes violence or hatred based on race, religion, gender, or orientation.
*   **Doxxing:** Publishing private information about individuals without their consent.
*   **Threats:** Using the platform to threaten or coordinate physical or digital harm.

## 4. Spam & Deception
*   **Bulk Messaging:** Using NexusVault issues or comments for commercial advertising or bulk spam.
*   **Misleading Information:** Impersonating other developers, organizations, or Luohino staff.

## 5. Compliance
Failure to comply with this policy may result in immediate account suspension and removal of content. If you discover a violation, please report it to **lutervyn@gmail.com**.
