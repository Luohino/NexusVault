# Environmental Impact & Sustainability

**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026

NexusVault recognizes that digital infrastructure has a significant environmental footprint. We are committed to minimizing the "Carbon Anomaly" of our software through intentional engineering and ethical partnerships.

## 1. Sustainable Infrastructure
We exclusively partner with cloud providers (Vercel, AWS, Supabase) that have made public commitments to 100% renewable energy usage. We favor data centers located in regions with the lowest carbon intensity.

## 2. Green Coding Standards
Our [Coding Standards](CODING_STANDARDS.md) prioritize energy efficiency:
*   **Minimal Payload Size:** We optimize assets and bundle sizes to reduce the energy required for data transmission.
*   **CPU Optimization:** We avoid resource-heavy background processes and prioritize efficient O(n) or better algorithms for repository indexing.
*   **Cache Strategy:** Aggressive caching at the Edge reduces the need for redundant server-side compute cycles.

## 3. Dark Mode by Default
The signature NexusVault Neo-Brutalist design utilizes high-contrast dark themes. This choice is intentionally made to reduce power consumption on OLED and modern display devices, extending battery life and reducing overall energy draw.

## 4. Digital Waste Reduction
We implement a rigorous [Data Retention Policy](DATA_RETENTION_POLICY.md) to ensure that stale, unused data is purged from our systems, reducing the physical hardware required to host the platform.
