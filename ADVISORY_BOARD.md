# User Advisory Board (UAB) Charter

The NexusVault User Advisory Board (UAB) is a select group of community members who provide strategic feedback on the platform's [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and governance.

## 1. Objectives
*   To represent the voice of the global developer community.
*   To review and provide feedback on experimental "Edge" features.
*   To assist in the prioritization of platform "Anomalies."

## 2. Membership
*   Members are appointed by the Founding Architect (Luohino) based on their contributions and technical expertise.
*   The board consists of 5-10 members serving rolling **1-year terms**.

## 3. Engagement
The UAB meets quarterly via encrypted digital channels to discuss the platform's strategic direction. Members are granted early access to unreleased features under the terms of the [NDA](NDA_TEMPLATE.md).

## 4. Non-Executive Role
The UAB serves in an advisory capacity only. Final decision-making authority remains with the [Council of Sentinels](GOVERNANCE_CHARTER.md).
