# Data Sharing Agreement (Template)

**Purpose:** Technical Research & Platform Improvement
**Provider:** NexusVault (Luohino)
**Recipient:** ____________________

## 1. Scope of Data
This agreement applies to the sharing of anonymized platform metadata or specific "Anomaly" datasets for the purpose of technical research or performance benchmarking.

## 2. Permitted Use
The Recipient shall use the shared data solely for the purposes defined in the research proposal and shall not attempt to de-anonymize or re-identify any NexusVault users.

## 3. Data Security
The Recipient must maintain rigorous technical and organizational measures to protect the shared data, as outlined in our [Cyber Hygiene Guidelines](CYBER_HYGIENE.md).

## 4. Deletion
Upon completion of the research or at the request of Luohino, the Recipient must permanently delete all copies of the shared data and provide written certification of destruction.

## 5. Public Disclosure
The Recipient must provide Luohino with a copy of any planned publication or research findings at least **14 days** prior to disclosure.
