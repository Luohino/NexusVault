# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy

**Effective Date:** April 25, 2026

NexusVault is committed to preventing the platform from being used for financial crimes. While we are currently a free service, we proactively establish this AML policy for any future paid tiers or sponsorship features.

## 1. Compliance Framework
We adhere to global AML standards, including the recommendations of the Financial Action Task Force (FATF).

## 2. Know Your Customer (KYC)
Before engaging in any high-value financial transactions (e.g., Enterprise Sponsorships), users must undergo a verification process. We utilize secure third-party identity providers to verify the legal status of organizations and individuals.

## 3. Monitoring & Reporting
We monitor financial transactions for suspicious activity, including:
*   Unusually large payments from high-risk jurisdictions.
*   Attempts to use anonymous or virtual payment methods where prohibited.
*   Rapid succession of multiple transactions from a single source.

## 4. Prohibited Transactions
NexusVault will not process payments from individuals or entities listed on international sanctions lists (e.g., U.S. SDN list).

## 5. Record Keeping
Financial records are maintained for a period of **7 years**, as required by international tax and AML laws.
